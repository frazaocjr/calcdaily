CALCULATION SCOPE
- Calculators use user-entered assumptions and do not assert country-specific tax, employment, overtime, contract, or platform rules.
- Invoice tax is an illustrative user-entered rate, applied to discounted services in the model.
- Late invoice interest uses simple interest with a 365-day year and a one-time fee.
- Gig earnings apply the entered platform fee to job earnings and incentives, excluding tips as a model assumption.
- Break-even projects are rounded up to whole projects.
