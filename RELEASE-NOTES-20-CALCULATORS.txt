IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- Income Tax and Paycheck calculators use user-entered flat percentages, not jurisdiction-specific tax tables or payroll rules.
- Retirement projections are mathematical scenarios, not guaranteed returns or investment advice.
- Calorie deficit output uses static energy-equivalent approximations and is not medical or dietary advice.
- Voltage Drop is a simplified resistive estimate and is not electrical design or code-compliance approval.
- Stair dimensions are preliminary geometry only and are not a building-code check.
- Material, paint, fuel, and electricity outputs depend on user-entered measurements, product specifications, prices, and rates.

BEFORE PUBLICATION
Review the example defaults and all assumptions for the markets you target. Verify tax, payroll, building, electrical, and consumer-safety requirements with reliable local sources. Confirm the live domain, HTTPS, redirects, contact email, and privacy disclosures before publishing.
