How the Board Foot Calculator Works

A board foot is a lumber volume unit equal to a board 12 inches long, 12 inches wide, and 1 inch thick. Enter dimensions in inches for thickness and width and in feet for length, then enter the number of pieces.

Formula and method

Board feet per piece = thickness (in) × width (in) × length (ft) ÷ 12. Total board feet = board feet per piece × number of pieces.

Tips for better estimates

Check whether a lumber quote uses nominal or actual surfaced dimensions and confirm how the supplier rounds board-foot quantities. Enter zero for price when you only need volume.

Important limitations

Board-foot totals are volume estimates, not a measure of usable finished yield. Defects, kerf, planing, grading, and supplier pricing conventions can affect the amount you buy and pay.

Board Foot Example: Calculate Board Feet for Lumber

For example, a piece measuring 2 inches thick, 6 inches wide, and 8 feet long contains (2 × 6 × 8) ÷ 12 = 8 board feet. Five identical pieces total 40 board feet. At an entered price of $4.50 per board foot, the estimated material cost is $180. Nominal lumber sizes may differ from actual dimensions, so check which measurements your supplier uses.
