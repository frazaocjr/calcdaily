How the Mulch Calculator Works

The calculator multiplies the bed length, width, and mulch depth, converts the volume to cubic meters and cubic yards, adds a material allowance, and divides by the bag volume you enter. The bag count is rounded up to a whole bag.

Formula and method

Volume = bed length × bed width × depth × (1 + allowance percentage). Bags needed = total volume ÷ volume per bag, rounded up.

Tips for better estimates

Measure the area to be covered and enter depth in inches for feet mode or centimeters for meter mode. Use the actual bag volume on the product label.

Important limitations

Mulch settles and compresses, and coverage differs by material type. The result is a shopping estimate, not a guarantee of final depth after settling.

How Much Mulch Do I Need? A Worked Example

A 10 ft × 5 ft garden bed with a 3-inch mulch depth needs 10 × 5 × (3 ÷ 12) = 12.5 cubic feet, or about 0.46 cubic yards, before adding an extra allowance. If each bag contains 2 cubic feet and you select 0% extra, 12.5 ÷ 2 = 6.25 bags, so you need to buy 7 whole bags. Measure the bag size from its label and allow for settling when planning your purchase.
