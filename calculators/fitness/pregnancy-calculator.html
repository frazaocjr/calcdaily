How Pregnancy Dating Is Estimated

Choose the date you know. When using the last menstrual period (LMP), the calculator adds 280 days to estimate the due date. When using a conception date, it adds 266 days. For gestational-age display, conception-based dating estimates the LMP as approximately 14 days earlier.

Clinical dating may differ

These calendar rules assume typical timing and do not account for every cycle pattern. A clinician may adjust dates using menstrual history, ultrasound, or other clinical information. The displayed trimester and week count are date-based estimates only.

Health note: This calculator does not confirm pregnancy or assess maternal or fetal health. Use prenatal care for diagnosis, dating, and decisions about care.

Practical question

How are pregnancy weeks counted?

Gestational age is typically dated from the first day of the last menstrual period rather than the presumed conception day. The calculator offers a calendar estimate; early ultrasound and clinical information may lead a clinician to revise the date.
