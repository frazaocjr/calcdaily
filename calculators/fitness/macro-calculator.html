How Macronutrient Grams Are Calculated

The calculator divides the calorie target across the selected protein, carbohydrate, and fat percentages. It converts those calorie allocations into grams using 4 calories per gram for protein, 4 for carbohydrates, and 9 for fat. The displayed gram amounts therefore depend on both the calorie target and the percentages selected.

Percentages and food choices

Check that the percentages add up to 100% if you want the full calorie target allocated. The result does not assess food quality, micronutrients, allergies, medical needs, or the suitability of a calorie target.

Health note: These numbers are planning estimates, not a prescribed diet. Individual nutrition needs vary, and people with medical or eating-related concerns should seek qualified guidance.
