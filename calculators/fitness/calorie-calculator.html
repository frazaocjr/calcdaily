How Daily Calorie Needs Are Estimated

The tool estimates resting energy with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation using age, sex, height, and weight. It multiplies that estimate by the selected activity factor to approximate total daily energy expenditure. The displayed maintenance and goal scenarios are then created by adding or subtracting fixed calorie amounts from that estimate.

Use goal figures cautiously

Real energy needs change with health, body composition, activity, and time. The displayed deficit and surplus options are mathematical scenarios—not a determination that a particular rate of weight loss or gain is safe, suitable, or sustainable for you.

Health note: Do not treat the most aggressive weight-loss option as a recommendation. People who are pregnant, under 18, have a medical condition, or have a history of disordered eating should seek individualized advice from a qualified professional.
