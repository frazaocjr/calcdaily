How the Next Period and Fertile Window Are Estimated

The next period date is estimated by adding the entered cycle length to the first day of the last period. The fertile-window estimate is then placed around an assumed ovulation date approximately 14 days before that predicted next period. The calendar uses the dates and average cycle length supplied.

Cycle tracking has important limits

Cycle length and ovulation timing can vary due to stress, illness, medication, hormonal changes, and many other factors. Recording several cycles may help describe a pattern, but it does not make a calendar prediction certain.

Health note: This tool is not a diagnostic tool and must not be used as a birth-control method. Seek medical advice for unusual bleeding, severe pain, missed periods, or fertility concerns.

Example and limitations

Calendar estimates are less reliable when cycles vary, are irregular, or are affected by health conditions, medication, or life stage. They should not be used as contraception or to diagnose a cycle disorder.

Can I use this period prediction for an irregular cycle?

The next-period date is projected from the last period and an average cycle length. Irregular cycles, stress, illness and hormonal changes can shift timing. The estimate is not a diagnosis or a reliable contraceptive method.
