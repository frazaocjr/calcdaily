Target Heart Rate Calculator

Estimate age-based maximum heart rate and exercise zones, with an optional calculation using resting heart rate.

Heart Rate Zones

Maximum Heart Rate: 190 bpm
Fat Burn (50 - 69%): 95 - 131 bpm
Cardio / Aerobic (70 - 84%): 133 - 160 bpm
Peak / Anaerobic (85 - 100%): 162 - 190 bpm

How the Heart-Rate Zones Are Estimated

The calculator first estimates maximum heart rate with the age-based equation 208 − (0.7 × age). When a resting heart rate is included and the heart-rate-reserve option is used, it applies the Karvonen approach: target heart rate equals resting heart rate plus a chosen fraction of the difference between maximum and resting heart rate. Otherwise, the displayed zones are percentages of estimated maximum heart rate.

Training zones are guides, not clinical thresholds

Age equations are population estimates and can differ substantially from an individual's measured maximum. Medication, heart conditions, fitness, heat, and fatigue can affect heart rate.

Health note: Stop exercising and seek appropriate medical help for concerning symptoms. Ask a healthcare professional before using target zones if you have a heart or respiratory condition or take medication that changes heart rate.
