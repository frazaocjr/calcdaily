How the Slope Calculator Works

Enter the x- and y-coordinates of two distinct points. The calculator subtracts the coordinates to find vertical rise and horizontal run, then calculates the line equation and angle. Vertical lines are identified separately because their slope is undefined.

Formula and method

Slope m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁). Y-intercept b = y₁ − m × x₁. Slope-intercept form: y = mx + b.

Tips for better estimates

Keep coordinate order consistent: x belongs with y for each point. A zero slope means a horizontal line; equal x-coordinates mean a vertical line.

Important limitations

This tool assumes a straight line through two distinct points. It does not perform regression or estimate a trend from a larger dataset.

Practical question

What if the two points have the same x-coordinate?

Slope is calculated as the change in y divided by the change in x. A vertical line has zero change in x, so its slope is undefined; a horizontal line has zero change in y and slope zero.
