How the Weighted Grade Calculator Works

Enter each assessment score and its percentage of the final course grade. Coursework weights plus the final-exam weight must total 100%. The calculator finds the current coursework average and the score needed on the final exam to reach your target.

Formula and method

Weighted coursework points = Σ(score × weight ÷ 100). Required final score = (target overall grade − weighted coursework points) ÷ (final exam weight ÷ 100).

Tips for better estimates

Copy weights from your syllabus and enter zero for unused assessment rows. If the required final score is above 100%, the target cannot be reached under the entered assumptions.

Important limitations

Schools may use letter grades, curves, dropped assignments, extra credit, or different rounding rules. Verify the calculation with your instructor or official grading policy.

Practical question

Why do assignment weights matter more than the raw number of scores?

A category worth 40% of the course grade affects the total more than a category worth 10%, even if both have the same number of assignments. Check your syllabus and remaining weights before estimating a needed final-exam score.
