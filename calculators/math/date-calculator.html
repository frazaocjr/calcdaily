How the Date Calculator Works

The tool compares two calendar dates without relying on the time of day. It reports the absolute number of calendar days, full weeks and remaining days, and weekdays between the dates. A separate adjustment adds or subtracts a whole number of days from the start date.

Formula and method

Calendar-day difference = end date − start date. Weekdays are counted one date at a time, excluding Saturdays and Sundays. Date adjustment = start date + entered number of days.

Tips for better estimates

Use ISO-style calendar dates and check whether the first or last day should be included for your particular deadline. Public holidays are not removed from the weekday count.

Important limitations

This is a calendar helper, not legal deadline advice. Business-day rules, public holidays, time zones, and inclusive counting conventions can differ by jurisdiction.

Practical question

Are weekdays the same as business days?

Not always. Weekday counts typically exclude Saturdays and Sundays, while business-day rules can also exclude public holidays and observe jurisdiction-specific schedules. Check which days your calculation includes before using it for a deadline.
