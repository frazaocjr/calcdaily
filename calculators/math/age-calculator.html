How Exact Age Is Calculated

The calculator compares the birth date with the current local calendar date and works through years, months, and days, borrowing from the previous month when necessary. It also reports the elapsed number of calendar days. Leap years and different month lengths are part of the calendar calculation.

Calendar age and total days

Years, months, and days describe calendar age. The total-day figure is calculated from the two calendar dates, so the time of day when you open the page does not add or remove a day. For official records, use the date conventions required by the relevant authority.

Worked example

For example, someone born on 4 November 2000 is 26 years old on 4 November 2026. On a date before the birthday, the calendar age remains one year lower until that birthday arrives.

Why is calendar age different from simply dividing days by 365?

Calendar age follows actual birthdays and month lengths, including leap years. Dividing a day count by an average year length will not always match the years, months and days shown on a calendar.
