How the Grade Point Average Is Calculated

The page provides three course rows. For each row, the calculator multiplies the selected grade-point value by the entered credit amount, adds the weighted points, and divides by the total credits across the rows. This is a weighted average: courses with more credits have more influence on the result.

Grading systems vary

The result depends on the grade-point scale and course credits selected in the form. Schools may use different scales, plus/minus values, pass/fail rules, repeated-course policies, or exclusions. Confirm your institution's rules before using the number for an application or academic decision.

Practical question

How does a credit-weighted GPA work?

Each entered grade value is multiplied by its course credit weight, and the results are divided by total credits. Grading scales vary across institutions; convert grades to the appropriate scale before entering them, and do not assume this is an official transcript calculation.
