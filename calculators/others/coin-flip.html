How the Virtual Coin Flip Works

Press the flip control to display one of two outcomes: heads or tails. The animation is a visual simulation of a coin toss and is intended for casual decisions or demonstrations of a two-outcome event.

Not an audited or security-grade draw

The virtual result is generated by browser-side pseudorandom logic. It is not a physical coin, certified randomization method, or auditable system for contests with legal or financial consequences.

Example and limitations

A fair two-sided coin has a theoretical probability of one half for each side over many independent flips. A short run can still contain streaks; the animation is for casual use, not an audited draw.
