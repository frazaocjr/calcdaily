How the Retirement Projection Works

The projection grows your current savings monthly and adds each monthly contribution at the end of the month. It then shows the nominal future balance and an inflation-adjusted estimate expressed in today’s purchasing power.

Formula and method

Monthly rate = (1 + annual return)^(1/12) − 1. Future value = starting balance × (1 + monthly rate)^months + monthly contribution × [((1 + monthly rate)^months − 1) ÷ monthly rate]. The zero-rate case uses contributions × months.

Tips for better estimates

Try conservative, middle, and optimistic return assumptions rather than relying on one forecast. Contributions are assumed to stay constant and occur at month-end.

Important limitations

This is an educational projection, not investment advice or a guaranteed return. It excludes fees, taxes, market volatility, contribution increases, employer matches, and withdrawals. Actual retirement outcomes can be materially different.
