How the Time Card Calculator Works

The calculator subtracts the clock-in time from the clock-out time, treats an earlier clock-out time as an overnight shift, and deducts the unpaid break. It converts the result to decimal hours and multiplies by the optional hourly rate to estimate gross pay.

Formula and method

Paid minutes = elapsed shift minutes − unpaid break minutes. Decimal hours = paid minutes ÷ 60. Estimated gross pay = decimal hours × hourly rate.

Tips for better estimates

Enter times using a 24-hour clock. If a shift crosses midnight, enter the next day’s clock-out time; the calculator recognizes that as an overnight shift.

Important limitations

This page handles one shift only. It does not calculate overtime, multiple breaks, shift differentials, tips, premiums, or legally required rounding. Confirm working-time and wage rules for your location.

Practical question

How do unpaid breaks affect a time card?

Work time is calculated from the entered start and end times after subtracting the unpaid break duration. Gross pay is then based on the hourly rate. The estimate does not determine employment-law overtime, meal-break compliance, payroll taxes or shift premiums.
