How the Tip and Split Are Calculated

The tip is the bill amount multiplied by the tip percentage. The calculator adds the tip to the entered bill to find the total, then divides that total by the number of people selected. This makes it easy to compare different tip percentages or group sizes before paying.

Choose the bill amount consistently

The tool applies the percentage to the amount you enter. It does not decide whether local etiquette or restaurant policy calls for calculating the tip before or after tax, and it does not account for service charges already included in the bill.

This is a convenience calculator; verify the receipt and local customs before settling the bill.

Example and limitations

The per-person split assumes the entered group size shares the bill and tip evenly. Adjust the group size or tip percentage when guests pay different amounts.

Restaurant Tip Calculator: Split a Bill Example

For an $80 restaurant bill with a 20% tip, the tip is $80 × 0.20 = $16 and the total is $96. If four people split the total equally, each person pays $24. The calculation applies the tip to the bill amount you enter; it does not automatically remove taxes, existing service charges, or other fees.
