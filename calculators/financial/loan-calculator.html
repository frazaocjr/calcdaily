How the Personal Loan Payment Is Calculated

The calculator treats the loan as a fixed-rate, fully amortizing loan with equal monthly payments. It converts the annual interest rate to a monthly rate, converts the term from years to months, and applies the standard payment formula. The total-payment result is the monthly payment multiplied by the number of installments; total interest is the amount above the original principal.

What is outside the model

Origination charges, account fees, insurance, late fees, variable rates, and early repayments are not included. Your lender's contract may use different day-count conventions or additional charges.

Financial note: Use this result as a comparison estimate only. Review the full annual percentage rate, fees, and repayment terms before borrowing.

Example and limitations

For a fixed-rate loan, the payment is determined by the principal, periodic interest rate, and number of payments. Fees, optional insurance, late charges, and variable-rate changes are not included unless entered separately.

What is included in the monthly personal-loan payment?

The estimated payment amortizes a fixed principal over the entered term at the assumed annual interest rate. It does not include origination charges, late fees, insurance or changing rates. Compare offers using the lender's disclosed APR and total repayment cost, not payment alone.
