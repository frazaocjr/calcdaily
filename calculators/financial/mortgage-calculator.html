How the Mortgage Payment Is Calculated

The estimated loan principal is the home price minus the down payment. The calculator converts the annual interest rate to a monthly rate and spreads principal and interest over the selected number of monthly payments using a fixed-rate amortization formula. The result is the scheduled principal-and-interest payment, not a complete housing budget.

Costs not included

Property taxes, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, homeowners association dues, closing costs, maintenance, and other local charges are excluded. Adjustable-rate mortgages, extra payments, and rate changes are not modeled.

Financial note: This estimate is not a mortgage offer or affordability assessment. Obtain a formal loan estimate and consider all housing costs before making a decision.

Scope: This estimate covers scheduled principal and interest only. It does not include property taxes, homeowners or mortgage insurance, HOA dues, closing costs, maintenance, or variable-rate changes.

Practical question

Does this mortgage payment include taxes and insurance?

No. This is a principal-and-interest estimate for a fixed-rate loan, based on price, down payment, term and annual rate. Property tax, homeowners insurance, mortgage insurance, association dues and closing costs can materially increase housing costs.
