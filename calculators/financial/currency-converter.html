How the Currency Conversion Works

The converter retrieves a reference-rate table with USD as its base currency. It converts the entered amount into the base and then into the selected target currency using the two rates. The page displays the provider's update timestamp when one is returned. The calculation is only as current as the external data successfully loaded by your browser.

Why your bank may show a different amount

Reference rates are not guaranteed transaction rates. Banks, card networks, remittance services, and exchange desks may apply their own spreads, commissions, cash rates, and settlement-time adjustments. The displayed amount does not include those charges.

Financial note: Use this tool for a quick comparison, not to price or execute a foreign-exchange transaction. Check the final rate and fees with the provider processing your payment.
