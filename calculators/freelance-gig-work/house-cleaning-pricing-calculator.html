How the House Cleaning Pricing Calculator Works

The estimate multiplies the area by your chosen rate, adds optional services and job costs, then applies the discount percentage to the subtotal.

Formula and method

Area charge = area × rate per area unit. Subtotal = area charge + add-ons + supplies + travel. Quote = subtotal × (1 − discount percentage).

Practical tips

Inspect the property and clarify what is included: number of bathrooms, kitchen condition, pets, deep-cleaning tasks, supplies, access, and cancellation terms.

Important limitations

The calculator does not set a market price or account for local minimum wages, insurance, taxes, labor time, property condition, or legal requirements. Rates and scope are supplied by the user.

Practical question

What should a house cleaning quote account for?

Floor area is only one pricing factor. The form can include supplies, travel and add-on work alongside the selected area rate. A final quote may also reflect property condition, time needed, local labour costs and customer expectations.
