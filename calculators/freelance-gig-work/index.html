Planning tools for independent work

Independent workers often need to price time that is not directly billable, cover operating expenses, understand platform deductions, and plan for periods with fewer bookings. These calculators let you test scenarios using the values you enter.

Use your own agreements and local rules

Rates, overtime, taxes, invoice charges, and platform fees vary by contract and location. The calculators do not determine employment status, legal entitlements, tax liability, or the fees charged by a particular marketplace. Check current agreements and local requirements before making financial decisions.

For pricing a job, compare the Hourly Rate, Day Rate, and Project Quote Calculators. Use the Invoice and Platform Fee Calculators to estimate what remains after charges.
