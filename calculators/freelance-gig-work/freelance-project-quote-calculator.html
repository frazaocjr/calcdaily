How the Freelance Project Quote Calculator Works

The estimate values planned work and revision time at your hourly rate, adds direct expenses, then applies the contingency allowance to that subtotal.

Formula and method

Labor = (estimated hours + revision hours) × hourly rate. Quote = (labor + direct expenses) × (1 + contingency percentage).

Practical tips

Define deliverables, deadlines, included revisions, payment milestones, and what counts as out-of-scope work. A calculator cannot replace a clear written agreement.

Important limitations

This is a pricing worksheet, not a market-rate recommendation or contract. It does not estimate client willingness to pay, withholding, sales tax, late payment risk, or currency conversion.

Practical question

Why add revision time and contingency to a project quote?

A project price based only on initial production hours can overlook review rounds, communication and scope uncertainty. The calculator helps model those costs, but the final contract should still define deliverables, revisions, payment terms and change requests.
