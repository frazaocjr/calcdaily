How the Freelance Day Rate Calculator Works

The monthly target and operating costs are grossed up using the reserve percentage. The required monthly revenue is then divided by the billable days you expect to work.

Formula and method

Required monthly revenue = monthly expenses + (desired monthly take-home income ÷ (1 − reserve rate)). Day rate = required monthly revenue ÷ billable days per month.

Practical tips

Use billable days rather than total weekdays. Account for preparation, travel, proposals, cancellations, and unpaid gaps when choosing the monthly number.

Important limitations

The tax reserve is only a user-entered percentage. Actual taxes, employment status, insurance, pensions, local rules, and client-specific pricing are not determined by this estimate.

Practical question

Why should a freelance day rate include nonbillable time?

Your quoted day rate must help cover business expenses, tax reserves and target take-home income across the days you can actually bill. Marketing, administration, holidays and gaps between projects reduce billable capacity and can push a sustainable rate higher.
