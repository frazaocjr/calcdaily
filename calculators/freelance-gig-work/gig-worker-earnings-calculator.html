How the Gig Worker Earnings Calculator Works

The calculator applies the selected platform fee to fares/job earnings plus incentives, adds tips, and subtracts distance-based vehicle costs and other expenses.

Formula and method

Fee = (job earnings + incentives) × platform fee rate. Vehicle cost = distance × operating cost per distance unit. Net estimate = job earnings + incentives + tips − fee − vehicle cost − other expenses. Net per hour = net estimate ÷ active hours.

Practical tips

Use a consistent period for earnings, distance, expenses, and hours. Include unpaid waiting time in active hours when comparing your effective hourly return.

Important limitations

Platform fee structures differ and can change. The model does not include depreciation schedules, financing, insurance, tax, statutory expenses, or platform-specific incentives unless entered by the user.

Practical question

Why can gross gig earnings overstate take-home pay?

Platform commissions, fuel, vehicle wear and other operating expenses reduce gross receipts. Compare net earnings with the hours worked, including waiting or travel where relevant, but remember that taxes and unpredictable future demand may still affect the result.
