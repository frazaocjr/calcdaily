How the Daily Worker Pay Calculator Works

Regular hours are paid at the base rate. Hours above the regular daily threshold are valued using the selected overtime multiplier. Allowances are added and advances are deducted.

Formula and method

Regular pay = days × min(hours per day, regular hours per day) × hourly rate. Overtime pay = days × max(hours per day − regular hours per day, 0) × hourly rate × multiplier. Estimated balance = regular pay + overtime pay + allowances − advances.

Practical tips

Record actual start and end times, breaks, agreed rates, and any advances. For irregular schedules, calculate each day separately before combining the amounts.

Important limitations

This is an arithmetic estimate only. It does not determine employee/contractor status, minimum wages, legally required overtime, holiday pay, deductions, benefits, or labor-law compliance in any country.

Practical question

How does the daily worker pay estimate handle overtime?

Enter workdays, hours, the base rate, an overtime multiplier and any adjustments. The estimate is a planning calculation: statutory overtime thresholds, minimum wages, taxes and employment classifications vary by jurisdiction.
