2. Calculator Inputs and Browser Processing

Most CalcDaily calculators perform their calculations in your browser. The values entered into those tools are not intentionally sent to a CalcDaily application server by the calculator code in this package. Do not enter sensitive information into any online tool unless you are comfortable with how that feature processes it. Your hosting provider may automatically process technical data such as IP address, browser type, requested page, and access time in server logs for security and operation.

3. External Services

The Currency Converter requests exchange-rate data from ExchangeRate-API's open.er-api.com endpoint. Your browser connects to that provider when the converter loads rates, and the provider may receive technical information associated with the request. The rates are reference data and are not a transaction quote.

The QR Code Generator uses a locally bundled encoder and processes the entered text or URL in the visitor's browser. The QR-generation feature does not send that content to a QR-generation provider. A generated QR image can still reveal its encoded contents to anyone who scans or receives it, so visitors should avoid encoding confidential information.

Email systems may retain messages according to their own policies and settings.
